Engineering Takeaway: Local-First State & Honest Gamification

By deriving progress, milestones, and reflections entirely from genuine state stored in the user's browser, NextStep maintains zero-friction privacy while delivering a rich, satisfying user experience without fabricated data, countdown urgency, or external backend requirements.
